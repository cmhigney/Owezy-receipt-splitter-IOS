Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Overview

Owezy ("we", "us", "our") is a receipt-splitting app. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights. By using Owezy you agree to these practices.

Information We Collect

  • Account info - Email address, display name, username, and optional phone number or payment handles.
  • Receipt data - Receipt photos, extracted text, and parsed line items used to process scans and split bills.
  • Split history - Records of past splits, items, amounts, and participants stored in your account.
  • Friends and payment details - Usernames, saved contacts, and optional payment details used to settle bills.
  • Local cache data - Temporary device-side storage for profile, history, friends, and scan state.

How We Use Your Data

  • Provide and operate the bill-splitting service.
  • Authenticate your account and verify your email address.
  • Process receipt scans using on-device OCR and, when needed, backend OCR providers.
  • Show non-personalized ads to free-tier users via Google AdMob.
  • Manage your subscription via RevenueCat.
  • Respond to support requests and communicate service changes.

Receipt Scanning and OCR

On iOS, Owezy first attempts on-device OCR using Apple Vision. If more help is needed to complete the parse, receipt text or the receipt image may be sent securely to Owezy's backend and Google Cloud OCR services. Receipt images are used only to complete the scan and are not stored as long-term account content.

Advertising

Owezy shows non-personalized ads to free-tier users using Google AdMob. The current ad flow does not rely on App Tracking Transparency permission for personalized advertising.

Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Google) - Authentication, database, and backend services. Data processed per Google's privacy policy.
  • Google AdMob - Non-personalized ad serving for free-tier users. Subject to Google's privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat - Subscription management. See revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • Apple Vision - On-device OCR used before backend fallback when available.
  • Google Cloud Vision / Document AI - Backend OCR processing used when receipt parsing requires cloud assistance.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only as necessary to operate core app features, such as syncing your account, processing receipt scans, managing subscriptions, showing ads, or helping you connect with other users by username and payment handle.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained while your account is active. To delete your account and associated data, use the account deletion option in Profile settings or contact us at support@owezy.app. We process deletion requests within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

Owezy is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Contact

Privacy questions? Email us at support@owezy.app.
