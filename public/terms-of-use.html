Owezy Pro is an auto-renewing subscription that increases your monthly scan allowance and removes ads. Pricing and billing periods shown in the app are provided by the App Store at purchase time.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your Apple account settings.
